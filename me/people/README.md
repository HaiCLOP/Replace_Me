# Notes on each person (generated)

`python -m replace_me research people` writes one `<chat_folder>.md` per chat here, and `research me` writes
`_me.md`, your master profile. They're plain markdown: read them, and fix anything wrong. A corrected note is the
fastest way to improve the copy with that person.

Each note has: who they are to you, how you talk to them, their world (people, places, in-jokes), what you like
and what annoys you, your small habits with them, what's going on lately, rules for the AI, and real exchanges
(the last section is never shown to the reply model: it would paste lines word for word).
